## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for university collaborative project spaces where academic focus meets peer warmth. It evokes calmness, intellectual clarity, and approachable companionship, eliminating the academic anxiety often induced by clinical productivity suites.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Soft Pastel Minimal**:

- **Tactile Softness**: Ample breathing room, plush radii, and delicate pastel accents replace aggressive visual hierarchies.
- **Academic Serenity**: Interfaces feel organized yet gentle, mimicking clean, high-grade stationery and modern study environments.
- **Clutter-Free Clarity**: Eliminates extraneous ornamentation, heavy gradients, glassmorphic blurs, and harsh dropshadows in favor of tranquil, tinted surfaces, deliberate alignment, and crisp dark slate typography.

## Colors

The palette is built entirely on muted, pastel-tinted surfaces and grounded by deep slate-navy ink to maintain rigorous readability and compliance without visually fatiguing students during prolonged group sessions.

- **Primary (`#7CA5B8` / Tonal Pastels `#E8F1F5`, `#D4E6F1`, `#BEE3F8`)**: Powder and sky pastels serve as the foundation for workspace navigation, project workspaces, and calm focal highlights.
- **Secondary (`#A78BFA` / Tonal Pastels `#EDE9FE`, `#E0E7FF`, `#E9D5FF`)**: Gentle lavender tones denote peer interactions, collaborative milestones, and asynchronous team activities.
- **Tertiary (`#F472B6` / Pastel Base `#FCE7F3`)**: Subdued pastel pink for personal badges, warm tags, and friendly invitations.
- **Background & Canvas (`#FAF9F6`, `#FDFBF7`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Soft cream paper tones that warm the digital interface, preventing cold-white glare.
- **Text & Foreground (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`)**: Deep navy and dark slate ink providing stark contrast, crisp typographic form, and maximum legibility against light backgrounds.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E2E8F0`)**: Whisper-weight neutral borders defining containers without harsh geometric interruption.
- **Strict Rule**: No dark mode, no saturated primaries, and no harsh fluorescents. Functional accents stay strictly within the desaturated pastel family.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric precision with gentle, open terminals, making academic collaboration legible, modern, and human.

- **Headlines**: Set tightly tracked (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with semi-bold or bold weights, delivering clear document and workspace orientation without visual heaviness.
- **Body Content**: Set with relaxed line heights (`line-height: 1.5` to `1.6`) to sustain comfortable reading for syllabi, project briefs, and long chat threads.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Formatted in medium and semi-bold weights for badges, status indicators, and course numbers, remaining legible even in small pastel containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid system framed by generous perimeter padding, cultivating an airy, stress-reducing interface.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `2.5rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers avoid hyper-dense packing; sidebars, kanban lanes, and study docs utilize wide `1.5rem` to `2.5rem` breathing gaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `2rem` outer margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Multi-pane project panels fold into tabbed or off-canvas views.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margin, `1rem` gutter. Component padding compresses from `space-xl` to `space-lg` to preserve screen efficiency.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is constructed through gentle tonal stacking and whisper-weight borders, strictly eschewing heavy drop shadows and glassmorphism.

- **Tonal Layering**: Base canvas sits at `#FAF9F6` or `#FDFBF7`. Primary modules, panels, and project cards sit atop on crisp `#FFFFFF` or pale pastel blocks (`#E8F1F5`, `#EDE9FE`, `#FCE7F3`).
- **Low-Contrast Outlines**: Cards, inputs, and list groups rely on `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders to provide tactile definition.
- **Ambient Micro-Shadows**: Only active popovers, dropdown menus, or interactive drag states may use a delicate ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Standard cards remain completely flat.

## Shapes

A deeply rounded shape language reinforces warmth, psychological safety, and effortless navigation.

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Feature generous `24px` (`rounded-3xl` / `1.5rem`) corners.
- **Inputs & Interactive Controls**: Form inputs, select dropdowns, and buttons feature `12px` to `16px` curvature.
- **Pills & Chips**: Micro-elements, status chips, and avatar highlights use fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`).

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - _Primary_: Background `#D4E6F1`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #BEE3F8`. On hover, transitions gently to `#BEE3F8`.
  - _Secondary / Ghost_: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E293B`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover shifts background to `#FAF9F6`.
  - _Accent_: Background `#EDE9FE`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E0E7FF`.
  - Corner radius is `12px` or pill-shaped (`9999px`); padding is `0.625rem 1.25rem`.

- **Cards**:
  - Encased in `24px` border radius (`rounded-3xl`) with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
  - Surface color is either solid `#FFFFFF` or very pale pastel washes (`#FDFBF7`, `#E8F1F5`).
  - Internal padding is generous (`space-lg` to `space-xl`). Never use heavy shadows.

- **Chips & Tags**:
  - Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `28px` to `32px`, horizontal padding `0.75rem`.
  - Subtle pastel surfaces based on contextual category: Course Tags (`#E8F1F5`), Role Tags (`#EDE9FE`), Deadline/Urgent (`#FCE7F3`). Text remains dark navy `#0F172A` for legibility.

- **Form Inputs & Fields**:
  - Border radius `12px` with `1px solid #E2E8F0` on white background.
  - Text styled with `body-md` in `#0F172A`. Placeholders in muted slate `#64748B`.
  - Focus state: Border transitions to `#7CA5B8` with a gentle `0 0 0 3px #D4E6F1` ring. No dark or high-contrast focus rings.

- **Checkboxes & Radio Buttons**:
  - Checkboxes use `6px` radius; radio controls use full circles.
  - Default: Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
  - Checked: Background `#7CA5B8`, border `#7CA5B8`, tick mark white `#FFFFFF`.

- **Lists & Task Rows**:
  - Flat items separated by `1px solid #F1F5F9` dividers or standalone padded rows with `12px` rounded corners.
  - Hover states tint softly with `#FAF9F6` or `#E8F1F5`.

- **Student Collaboration Components**:
  - _Teammate Avatars_: Stacked avatar circles with `2px` white outline rings and pastel fallback backgrounds.
  - _Milestone Trackers_: Pill-shaped progress meters with a `#E2E8F0` track and a `#7CA5B8` or `#A78BFA` pastel fill.
